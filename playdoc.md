## 2d Crawler Ideation
    - top-down
    - playable character
    - input: 4 directions

Concepts
    - player represented by a shape in the middle
    - player's turn
    - square room
    - 4 doors spread equally
    - choosing a door leads to events
    - event is a sequence of interactive activities

    - event outcomes:
        - death
        - next room
        - upgrades / economy

Motivation
    - all 4 doors launch a different type of events
    - event is a sequence of exploration and mini-games
    - valuables: upgrades
    - reward / punishment
